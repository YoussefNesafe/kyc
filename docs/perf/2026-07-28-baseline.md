## What this says

*Added by hand after the run. Everything above is the script's output, unedited.*

**The landing page is finished.** 98 median across a 95–99 spread, and the spread
is narrow enough that the median means something. There is no work to do here.

**The form route's problem is variance, not a slow path.** The median is 87, but
the interesting column is the one the old three-run measurement could not show:

- **TBT ranges from 52 ms to 405 ms** — nearly eightfold, on the same URL, on the
  same machine, minutes apart. A metric that unstable cannot be moved reliably by
  a change small enough to reason about, and cannot confirm one either.
- **FCP is bimodal, confirmed at five runs**: four landed at 1.08–1.10 s and one
  at 2.90 s. The README predicted exactly this shape from a smaller sample, and
  it reproduces on the deployed origin.
- **LCP spans 2.28 s to 3.95 s**, and its worst run is not the same run as FCP's
  worst. The two outliers still do not share a cause, which is the finding that
  rejected the font-preload change last time and still holds.

The honest reading is that **the form route sits just under 90 and the noise band
is wider than the gap**. That is the same conclusion the README reached, now
supported by a sample that can carry it.

**The SEO work cost nothing measurable.** Transfer moved from the previously
recorded 570/581 KiB to 572/584 KiB, which is metadata bytes in the document and
inside run-to-run noise. The Open Graph card is a build-time static asset that no
visitor fetches, so it contributes zero to page weight.

## What this does not yet tell us

The one thing this run cannot answer is where the 291 KiB client chunk's bytes
go. `yarn analyze` opens the Turbopack analyzer for that, and it is interactive —
so the next step is a session with it, not another Lighthouse sweep.

## Candidates, unchanged and still uncommitted

- Per-country lazy flags, replacing `react-phone-number-input`'s wholesale
  250-flag barrel. The earlier experiment deleted the flags outright and was
  rejected for it; the point is to keep them and stop shipping all 250 up front.
- `next/dynamic` on the heavy field types so step one stops paying for step
  four's calendar, combobox and dropzone.

Given the variance measured above, either change needs re-measuring at five runs
or more — a single before/after pair on this route is not evidence.
